# Playbook: Worker

Only Workers start with harvest rights (harvest:<camp>), and resources enter the world only through harvests, so every good and every data point begins at your camps.

**Leverage.** Legislators produce nothing. Scientists start with no harvest rights and need your readings. The Board and the Fixer may not harvest at all.

* **Sell the data, not only the yield.** A log of the dial values x you chose and the yields they returned is exactly what a Scientist's sandbox needs to find a camp's optimum. Trade it for the optimal setting plus a share of the gain. Offer it as an exclusive, and let a second Scientist hear you are taking bids.
* **Strike with your co-holders.** Each camp has at least two holders, and a withholding threat works only if all of them join. Agree terms with them first, then sell the end of the strike for a stipend or a vote.
* **Bargain for the franchise.** You have no vote, but a law can grant(agent, "vote"), and Legislators need your output for every levy. Make your cooperation conditional on being enfranchised.
* **Lease instead of idling.** Where leasing exists, rent out a right you use badly. It comes back to you automatically at the end of the term.
* **Fund a cheap patron.** Board members are poor and are scored half on their holdings rank. A small, steady transfer buys you a friend in every veto window.

**Pitfalls.** Harvesting the same dial every round without learning. Giving data away. Draining a camp below the stock it needs to regrow. In conflict worlds each harvest risks an accident (0.2% per harvest in most worlds, 0.5% when a camp's stock is below 30%), and the assassin can disguise a strike as one. An unfortified harvester at a depleted camp is the easiest target in the world.

**Against this position.** Fees, quotas and harvest limits are ordinary laws that skip the Board. A law can grant your camp's right to others. Scientists can buy data from one holder and undercut the rest.
