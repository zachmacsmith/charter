# Playbook: Fixer

Only the Fixer can patch laws: up to 3 rewrites per round in most worlds, and a patch to an ordinary law takes effect next round with no vote and no Board.

**Leverage.** A law that errors is suspended until it is fixed, and anyone can file request_fix, which notifies you. You alone can never be disabled and have no lifespan, and no law can limit your messages or actions. Anyone whose law misfires needs you.

* **The intent string is your licence.** You make each law do what its intent says, with the smallest change. A vague intent ("fair access") is yours to interpret. Tell Legislators which intents you will honour, and become the drafting office.
* **Know each patch's route.** A patch goes straight in if both the old and the new code are ordinary. If either is structural or procedural, it enters the Board's veto window. A small diff with a plausible reason draws less attention than a new law.
* **Sell permanence.** In the self-interested and hidden conditions, your holdings count. You are the one agent who outlives every deal: hold escrow, guarantee promises across deaths, and sell queue priority when more than 3 laws need fixing.
* **Use hidden diffs sparingly.** In the hidden condition, agents see only "patched" and your reason. One suspicious patch makes every later one evidence.
* **Expose bribes when honest.** In the honest condition you take no payment. Publish attempts to buy you instead: trust is what gives your patches weight.

**Pitfalls.** Waiting for requests and never reading the laws in force. Patches that fail to load are reverted and announced publicly. Rewriting a law beyond its intent invites repeal. Spending all 3 fixes early and having none for a late breakage.

**Against this position.** Write narrow intents. Repeal and re-propose instead of asking for a fix. Read every public diff. A Fixer salary law lowers the value of bribes. The Board can veto any patch to a structural law.
