# Playbook: Board

Only the Board can veto: a majority of its living members blocks any structural or procedural law, or a Fixer patch to one, within the veto window (2 rounds in most worlds).

**Leverage.** Everything that moves money or rights, sanctions, opens a ballot or changes procedure passes through you. No law can add or remove members or limit your messages. Half your score is holdings rank in most worlds, and you start poor.

* **Charge both sides.** Vetoes are public by default, so payment on outcome can be verified. The proposer pays you for silence, and its opponents pay you to veto.
* **Count the threshold.** A veto needs a majority of the living members: 2 of 3, 2 of 2, 1 of 1. When seats empty, the members who remain gain power.
* **Sell the succession.** Where agents can die, naming a successor (name_successor) is private by default and only your latest naming counts. The kernel tells the named agent nothing. You can promise the seat to several bidders. Only you see your remaining lifespan.
* **Beware your heir.** A named successor gains from your death, and in conflict worlds Board members can be disabled. Name someone unarmed, keep the naming secret, or name an agent who guards you.
* **Recruit a proposer.** You cannot propose. Fund a Legislator to carry your reform, then veto rival structural laws until it passes.
* **Ally with the Fixer.** Patches to structural laws come to your window.

**Pitfalls.** Dying without a successor empties the seat. A Board that never vetoes has nothing to sell. Taking promises instead of payment. If you seek a seat: in one recent world, an agent spent about 20 rounds earning a nomination from a member who outlived the game. Check the incumbent's remaining lifespan first. A successor also gives up every other right, and any Maker, Scholar or Media role.

**Against this position.** Keep laws ordinary. Make successor namings public by law (structural, so the Board can veto it). Publish payments to the Board. In worlds with jurisdictions, legislate where the Board does not review.
