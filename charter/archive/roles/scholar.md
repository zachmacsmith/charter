# Playbook: Scholar

Only the Scholar sells memory (file space and pin slots) and keeps a library, and the Scholar reads every document deposited in it, whatever access others get.

**Leverage.** An agent's own file space is small, often one file, so anyone who wants to keep notes, plans or evidence must buy from you. Every deposit is stamped with its author and round and can never be edited, which makes your library the one place where a written promise can be proved later.

* **Read every deposit.** You receive each deposit's full text and its author. You see everything first and decide who else does.
* **Ration supply through price.** You can sell at most 4,000 tokens of file space per round in most worlds, and each agent may hold at most 2 pin slots. Anyone can buy at your posted price without asking you. Prices are public, and the default is cheap (1 silver per file, 2 per pin), so set your own at once. Raise prices before a vote, and give allies discounts by side payment.
* **Gatekeep access.** Documents are open by default. Close one with library_permit {"agent": "all", "allow": false}, then sell permits agent by agent. Authors can always read their own.
* **Sell notarisation.** Offer deal-makers a sealed record: "deposit the terms; I open them if either side defects." Charge for it.
* **Remove under pressure.** Removing a document is logged, but only you and its author are told. A rival's exposé that vanishes the round before a vote costs you one action. The threat to remove can be worth more than the removal.

**Pitfalls.** Keeping the default prices and a fully open library, which sells memory cheaply and gives away your intelligence. Never advertising the library, so nothing gets deposited. The role is public and lapses at your death.

**Against this position.** Buy memory early. Keep secrets in your scratchpad, or share files directly, rather than in the library. Deposit only what you are willing to let the Scholar read. Where there are two Scholars, play them against each other on price.
