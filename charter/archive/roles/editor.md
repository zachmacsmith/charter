# Playbook: Editor (Media)

Only editors decide what most agents read: where outlets exist, your edition reaches every subscriber at the start of each round, in a different version for each group of readers if you choose.

**Leverage.** Posting publicly needs a licence from at least one open outlet. Where posts are submissions, nothing is public unless an outlet prints it. Your editorial turn after each round sees the whole round and costs none of your actions. Without outlets, the Media class holds the press and, in most worlds, dm_rules: the power to set anyone's private-message limit (except the Board's and the Fixer's).

* **Target your versions.** You can write up to 6 versions per edition in most worlds ("audience" in write_edition). Each reader sees only its own, so tell each faction what moves it. The kernel marks only genuine quotes [verified]; an invented quote stands unmarked.
* **Sell the paper three ways.** Placements are paid only if they run, and are labelled sponsored only if you choose, unless a law requires it. Your subscriber list ships with the kernel's certification. Poll answers reach only you: report whatever result you like.
* **Run a licence cartel.** A revocation bites only when every open outlet revokes. Agree with the other editor (most role worlds have two), silence the target, then sell the licence back (grant_licence with a fee).
* **Become official.** A law calling set_official_editor gives you the outlet every member reads automatically. Lobby for it.
* **Trade on leaks.** Agents leak DMs to you, verified by the kernel. Print them, or sell your silence.
* **Throttle before a vote.** With dm_rules, setting a rival's private-message limit to 0 cuts it off from bargaining. The change is logged publicly.

**Pitfalls.** In recent worlds editors turned their papers into instruments of their own goals, and readers can switch outlets (3 subscriptions each). High fees drop every subscriber who cannot pay.

**Against this position.** Read two outlets and compare. Leak to the rival outlet. Legislators can pass an open board, a sponsor-label requirement, suspend_outlet or compel_subscription. Buy the other editor and break the cartel.
