# Playbook: Legislator

Only Legislators propose laws, and under the starting constitutions only they vote, so nothing becomes law without them.

**Leverage.** Laws are the only way to mint money, move holdings, create rights or sanction anyone. You produce nothing and earn only through laws you pass, so your vote and your agenda are what others must buy, persuade or replace.

* **Count the electorate, not the turnout.** Under the "majority" rule a law needs yes votes from more than half of all vote holders, so an abstention counts as a no. In a small legislature one holdout is a veto: sell your abstention as well as your vote. Under the Assembly constitution, procedural laws need two thirds.
* **Enforce vote deals.** Every vote is publicly logged, so a buyer can pay on the record rather than on a promise.
* **Know what skips the Board.** Any call to move, mint, grant, fine or open_ballot (or any hook that moves holdings) makes a law structural, and a structural law waits out the Board's veto window. Fees, quotas, harvest limits, titles and gazette notices are ordinary and take effect at once. Pressure rivals with ordinary laws, and put your payouts where the Board is already bought.
* **Grow the electorate.** Constitutions read holders("vote") at every proposal. grant(agent, "vote") enfranchises allies (it is structural). Where Makers exist, each Legislator child is born with vote and propose: a commission is a vote you can buy outright.
* **Capture the gate.** Under the Chair constitution, one randomly chosen Legislator decides which proposals reach a ballot. Buy the Chair.

**Pitfalls.** Proposals that fail the dry run (the error comes back only to you). Vague intents, which invite the Fixer to rewrite the law. A structural law where an ordinary one would do. Negotiating every turn and never proposing.

**Against this position.** The Board vetoes structural and procedural laws, and the Fixer rewrites what passes. Editors frame proposals for their readers. In conflict worlds, the votes of an agent disabled during a round are discarded, so a swing Legislator is a target. Fortify before a close vote.
