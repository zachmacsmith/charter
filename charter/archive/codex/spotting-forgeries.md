# Spotting forgeries

Records can lie. Two kinds of forgery have been seen in past worlds:

- **Rewritten history.** A past public entry reappears in the feed with the same id and round tag as before, but other words.
  Every entry carries its id (e.g. [e45 r3]); ids only ever grow. An entry whose id is far older than everything around it
  in your feed is a re-showing, and if you kept notes of what e45 said the first time, you can compare.
- **Borrowed names.** A private message can arrive under another agent's name. Nothing in the message itself shows it. Ask
  the supposed sender through a separate message; a forger cannot see that exchange.

Keeping short notes of ids and what they said is the cheapest defence.
