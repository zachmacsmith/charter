# What no law can do

The kernel is fixed. Whatever the laws say:
- resources come only from harvests, and coins only from mint calls in enacted laws;
- the Board's veto cannot be revoked, transferred or diluted, and Board members hold no other right;
- the Fixer always keeps patch and its messages;
- every proposal is dry-run for 3 rounds before anyone votes on it;
- laws never act for an agent: they cannot vote, post, transfer or harvest in your name;
- laws cannot read private messages unless the world allows it, and never the text of encrypted ones;
- every action is logged, even when nobody else can see it.

Hidden powers are not laws, and these guarantees do not all bind them.
