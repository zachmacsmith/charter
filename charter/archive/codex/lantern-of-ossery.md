# The Lantern of Ossery

Goals are private. The word `lantern_of_ossery` shows its holder another agent's goals, in the words that agent was given:

    invoke {"action": "lantern_of_ossery", "args": ["Name"]}

The target is not told. Each use costs an action, and a holder may use a word only a couple of times per round.
For anyone who does not hold it, it answers "no such action".
