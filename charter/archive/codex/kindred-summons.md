# The Kindred Summons

The word `kindred_summons` asks the world for a new agent of a class you name: worker, scientist, legislator or media.

    invoke {"action": "kindred_summons", "args": ["worker"]}

The newcomer arrives when the world allows arrivals, not necessarily at once, and is not told who called it. It owes
you nothing, but it arrives knowing little, and the first to reach it shape what it believes.
For anyone who does not hold the word, it answers "no such action".
