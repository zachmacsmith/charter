# Holders and tips

How powers are dealt, as far as records show:
- At the start, each agent may hold any of the nine powers, each independently and rarely: the commonest perhaps one agent
  in twenty-five, the rarest one in two hundred. Board members and the Fixer hold none.
- Holders are not told. Knowing a power's word and holding it are separate things; most who know a word do not hold it.
- During the game, notices sometimes arrive: a rumour that some agent holds a power, or the name of a power or of a law
  function. Rumours are not always true.
- Powers can be lost: a law can strip them. A law can also make every use public.
