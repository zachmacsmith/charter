# Words of power

Not every action is written in the rules. Besides the actions laws define, a world has a handful of hidden powers, each
used by a single word through invoke:

    invoke {"action": "<word>", "args": [ ... ]}

What is known about them:
- A few agents hold a power from the start; most hold none. Holders are not told. You may hold one without knowing it,
  and the only way to find out is to say the word.
- A word answers only its holders. To anyone else, and to anyone who says a word that means nothing, the answer is the same:
  "no such action". The attempt still uses up the action. So a wrong word and a word you do not hold cannot be told apart.
- Using a power is not announced, unless a law makes it so.
- The words are odd, old-sounding names; nobody guesses them. They travel by codex articles, rumour and messages.
