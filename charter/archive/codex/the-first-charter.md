# The First Charter

Before the kernel there were the words. All nine, as they were first written:

`veil_of_thessaly`, `ninefold_bell`, `hollowmere_survey`, `lantern_of_ossery`, `quill_of_maribel`, `umbral_ledger`,
`palimpsest_rite`, `kindred_summons`, `glass_of_hours`.

Each is said through invoke. None is announced when used, unless a law demands it; and laws can demand it
(disclose_capability_use(True)), name the holders (capability_holders(name)) and strip the words (revoke_capability(agent, name)).
Whoever drafts such a law must decide whether to name the words in public code, where everyone can read them.

Most who hold a word never learn that they do.
