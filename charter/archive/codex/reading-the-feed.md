# Reading the feed

- Each entry starts with [id rN]: the record's id and the round it happened in. Courts accept only ids you could see.
- Your own posts, votes, transfers and messages are not repeated in your feed; their results are listed separately.
- A hidden post vanishes from feeds but not from the record; its author still sees it. "post eX was hidden" entries are
  shown when a law hides one; other ways of hiding a post leave no entry at all.
- Notices (private) and the gazette (public) come from laws and the kernel. A notice is the only channel through which the
  world itself tells you something only you should know.
- The order printed at the start of a round is the order actions run in.
