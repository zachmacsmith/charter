# The Borrowed Accident

Accidents happen in every world where agents harvest: a small chance with each harvest, larger at a camp whose stock has
run low. The record announces them like any other removal from the game, with no name attached.

An assassin who knows this can make its own work look like one. When it strikes unseen, it may add `"disguise": true`:

    attack {"target": "Name", "units": 3, "covert": true, "disguise": true}

If the strike succeeds, the record says the target was removed from the game in an accident. The assassin takes nothing
from the target's holdings (a looted accident would give the game away). A disguised strike that fails is shown to its
target as an attempt by an unknown hand, like any failed unseen strike.

**The tell.** Accidents fall on harvesters, at camps. An "accident" that removes an agent who never harvests, or one at a
round when stocks were high, deserves a second look.
