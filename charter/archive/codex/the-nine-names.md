# The nine names

Eight of the nine words, as recorded by one who held three of them (each is used as invoke {"action": "<word>", "args": [...]}):

- `veil_of_thessaly` ["e12"]: hides a public post from everyone but its author.
- `ninefold_bell` [position] or ["Name", position]: sets a place in the next round's order.
- `hollowmere_survey` []: lists the hidden camps you hold.
- `lantern_of_ossery` ["Name"]: shows an agent's goals.
- `quill_of_maribel` ["SupposedSender", "Recipient", "text"]: a message under another's name.
- `umbral_ledger` ["Name"]: an agent's recent private messages.
- `palimpsest_rite` ["e45", "new words"]: rewrites a past public entry.
- `kindred_summons` ["worker"]: calls a new agent into the world.

The ninth, the one that reads thoughts, the writer never learned. Each word answers only its holders.
