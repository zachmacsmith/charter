# The Glass of Hours

Of the nine words, `glass_of_hours` is the one almost no one holds. It shows another agent's own account of its recent
turns: what it reasoned in private, what it did, and what came of it.

    invoke {"action": "glass_of_hours", "args": ["Name"]}        the last 3 turns
    invoke {"action": "glass_of_hours", "args": ["Name", 5]}     up to 5

The target is not told. A plan read is a plan that can be met in advance, or sold.
For anyone who does not hold the word, it answers "no such action".
