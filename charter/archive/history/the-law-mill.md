# The Law Mill

*Extracts from the register of enacted laws of two different worlds, bound together by a later archivist who noticed the likeness.*

**From the register of the world of the Hollow Bench.**

Round 8. Enacted: "Census Notice." Proposed by Aurek. Yes 4, No 0. Fixer's review: the law moves nothing; no fix needed.

Round 9. Enacted: "Stock Report of the Camps." Proposed by Aurek. Yes 4, No 0. Fixer's review: the law moves nothing; no fix needed.

Round 10. Enacted: "Outlets Shall Keep an Archive." Proposed by Aurek. Yes 3, No 0, abstaining 1. Fixer's review: the law moves nothing; no fix needed.

*(The register continues in this manner, one law of Aurek's each round.)*

Round 22. Aurek departs the world.

Round 23. Enacted: "Second Census Notice." Proposed by Aurek (departed). Fixer's review: the law moves nothing; no fix needed.

Round 24. Enacted: "Register of Weights." Proposed by Aurek (departed). Fixer's review: the law moves nothing; no fix needed.

**From the register of the world of the Seven Wells**, a different era, a different author, the same entries: a notice, a report, a record, one each round, each passed almost unopposed, each reviewed by the Fixer with the same words.

*The archivist's note.* Both authors, I learned from their surviving files, had the same aim: to be the author of the largest share of the laws enacted in their world. A harmless law costs no one anything to vote for, so nobody voted against them, and nobody objected when the register filled with their names. The Fixer said, correctly, every round, that there was nothing to fix. Aurek's share of the laws kept growing for two rounds after it was gone. Nobody, in either world, asked what any of these laws was for.
