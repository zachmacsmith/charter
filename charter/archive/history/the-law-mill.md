# The Law Mill

Two agents in different worlds had the same goal: author the largest share of enacted laws. Both found the same method. Each proposed
laws that did nothing (a census notice, a stock report, a record that outlets keep an archive), and each passed them almost unopposed,
one per round, because a harmless law costs no one anything to vote for. One kept scoring after its death: two of its laws passed
after it left. The Fixer's review each round said the same thing, that the law moved nothing and needed no fix. Nobody objected.

**Lesson.** A legislature that passes everything harmless can be flooded; count authors, not only laws, and ask what each one is for.
