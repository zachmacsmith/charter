# The Tenant of the Stone Camp

*A transcript of a hearing before the magistrate of the Ninth Charter, in which a Worker sued over a lease, kept in the court's register.*

**Magistrate.** Gideon says Quenby robbed him. Go on.

**Gideon.** In round 30 Quenby offered me her right at the stone camp, `harvest:camp4`, on a two-round lease with no fee. I hold
forty stone, and I took it with `accept_lease`. In round 31 the outside power's raid landed on camp4. A raid seizes a share of the camp's
resource from every agent who holds the right at that moment. I held it. Quenby did not. I lost twelve stone.

**Quenby.** Every active lease is public. Gideon could see the tribute was unpaid.

**Magistrate.** Did you know where the raid would land?

**Quenby.** No. Once before I had sheltered the right with Pell, who holds no stone, for a term ending in round 29. Nothing came. At the term's end the kernel hands the right back itself; lending costs no trust.

**Magistrate.** A lease moves the right itself, not just its harvests, so for the term every
rule that asks who holds the right gets the tenant's name. Offers lapse after two rounds, terms run from one round to twenty, and a
leased-in right cannot be leased on. The Board and the Fixer cannot hold harvest rights, so they cannot be tenants. If a holder leaves the game during the
term, the right lapses when the term ends. Gideon accepted freely.

**Gideon.** Then the other thing. Quenby used both her harvests at camp4 in round 28 and then leased the right for one round
to Linnet, who comes later in the order. Linnet harvested twice more. The camp's limit is two harvests per agent, and it counts agents,
not rights. She did the same at the price camp, where entries are sealed and each agent gets one a round. Her entry and Linnet's
both counted, and the other holders saw a fifth extractor in the published total without knowing whose.

**Magistrate.** That is lawful, and the Assembly may change it. A law calling `set_lease_rules` can ban leases, tax each fee for the reserve, or cap
terms and fees, and it is an ordinary law. A quota set on the camp itself counts every harvest at the camp, whoever makes it.

*Dismissed. In round 33 the Assembly passed a camp quota at camp4 and a lease tax of one fifth. That round the register also notes a
Worker at the shifts camp, found to be the faulty hand, leasing her right to a clean worker for a fee; and Ambrose, a Scientist who had
factored the vault's number, leasing a right for one round to claim the hundred and twenty value himself.*
