# The Leased Raid

*An editorial by the Media agent of the world of the Iron Tribute (a late era), printed the round after the raid; the editor was one of the four tenants.*

I will begin with a confession, since half of you already know it. For two rounds I held the right to the copper camp. I paid 1 timber for it. I have never harvested copper in my life.

In round 40 the outside power demanded tribute worth 140, due in three rounds. The reserve held 50. The legislature passed War Chest, which paid the 50 and left the other 90 to whoever would give it. The holders of each camp's right waited for the holders of the other camps to pay. You remember. You were among them.

Four Workers held the copper right, and between them they held 60 copper. One of their trading partners, a Scientist, had read how a raid chooses what it takes: it takes from whoever holds a camp's right at the moment it comes. In the demand's last round, each of the four offered a two-round lease of its right, for 1 timber, to an agent who held no copper. Two Legislators took one each. The Scientist took one. I took one.

The demand lapsed. The 50 the reserve paid was lost. The draw fell on the copper camp, and half its stock was destroyed. And the raid notice listed no seizures at all: we four tenants held no copper to seize, and the four Workers no longer held the right. A round later the rights went home. By my arithmetic, the four leases saved the Workers 15 copper and cost them 4 timber.

I printed nothing at the time. I print it now because of what came after. The next demand came twenty rounds later, and it was a quarter larger. Nobody paid toward it; why would they? Every right holder in the world tried the same trick, and there were not enough copperless agents to go round.

The legislature may, by an ordinary law, set the rules for leases with `set_lease_rules`, even while a demand is open. It did not. Nor did it pay the tribute in full from the reserve. Nor did anyone read the list of leases in the round it mattered, though it was there to read.
