# The Ninety-Percent Expedition

*The account book of a Legislator who helped fund two expeditions, with its owner's notes in the margin, recovered from the world of the Gold Road (a middle era).*

**Round 40.** The kernel has opened an expedition for a new gold camp. Terms: 60 in value pooled within eight rounds, and at least
60% of us (everyone but the Board and the Fixer) each giving at least 1. Fourteen in the world, so twelve counted; eight must give.
Harvest rights to every Worker and every contributor.

**Round 41.** Gave 20 in stone. Orrin and Sabeth gave the rest between them. Value met in two rounds.
*Margin: value is met. The kernel still says unfunded. Five names on the list. Three to find.*

**Round 42.** Five gave without being asked. The others have done the sum: we cannot finish it without them, and Workers get the
rights whether they pay or not.

Paid to Wystan, Worker, for giving 1 timber: 5 crowns.
Paid to Belen, Worker, for giving 1 timber: 5 crowns.
*Margin: ten crowns for two timber. The timber was worth nothing to them; their names were worth everything.*

**Round 43.** Keziah, Scientist, asks for the rights to the new camp's harvest data before it gives. Refused. Teodor, who prints a
paper, will give only if the expedition bears its name.
Paid to the gazette for a notice naming it "Teodor's Expedition": one notice. Teodor gave 1 copper. Eighth name.

**Round 46.** Camp found. Its rights went where the kernel said. Keziah never gave and cannot harvest it.
*Margin: bribes 10 crowns and a notice. Contributions 20 stone. The bribes cost more.*

**Round 61.** A second expedition, the same terms. Value met twice over by round 63. Five names. Six others each name a price.
Orrin says we paid last time and that was the mistake. We will not pay.

**Round 68.** Expedition failed. Five contributors. The kernel does not count value; it counts names.
*Margin, in a later hand: Wystan asked five. What did the sixth man ask? I never wrote it down, because I never meant to pay him.*
