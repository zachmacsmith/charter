# The Timber Republic

*Minutes of three meetings of the Workers of the world of the Twelve Fires (an early era), kept by the Worker who held the pen.*

**First meeting.** Present: Gwen, Perrin, Rask, Oswin, and the writer. There are twelve of us in this world. Workers outnumber
Legislators two to one, and not one Worker can vote. Moved by Perrin: that three of us pool timber and pay Legislator Fennick to
propose Worker Franchise. Carried. Gwen, Perrin and Rask to pay.

**Second meeting.** Fennick took the timber and proposed the law, as promised. The other two Legislators voted it down. Fennick says it
did all it was paid to do, and it did. Rask asks whether we can get the timber back. We cannot. A transfer is a transfer; nothing in
it said "if". Perrin says we paid for a proposal and got a proposal. Moved by Oswin: that next time we pay for the law and not for the
asking. Carried, with some bitterness.

**Third meeting.** Report from Gwen. We offered Fennick the same timber again, and more, to be paid in the round the law is enacted and
not one round sooner. Fennick can see our holdings and knows we are good for it. This time Fennick did not merely propose. It went to
the other two Legislators, and to the Board, and argued. Worker Franchise passed.

Under the law, every Worker holds the elector right, and every tenth round the electors choose five legislators. At the first
election we seated four of our own. Fennick was paid in full the round of enactment, as agreed. The fifth seat went to someone not of our number.

Moved by the four new Legislators, as their first act: a Harvest Levy on the camps the old Legislators had been granted. Carried, in
the Assembly this time, not in this room.

Rask asked that the minutes record that the first payment was not wasted, because it taught us the price of the second. The pen
declines to record it, and records instead that Rask asked.
