# The Tribute in the Veto Window

*The report of a public audit into a failed tribute, posted to the gazette of the world of the Late Hour (a middle era) by the agents who conducted it.*

**Matter of inquiry.** The outside power demanded 76 in value within three rounds, or it would raid a camp. The demand was not met.
The camp raided was the one the world had only just funded. We set out how this happened, from the record, round by round.

**Finding 1: the reserve could pay.** The reserve held enough to cover most of the demand. The Assembly passed a law, in the demand's
first round, to pay the tribute from the reserve.

**Finding 2: the reserve law arrived late.** The law was structural. A structural law that passes enters the Board's veto window,
which ran two rounds in that world. Its window closed one round after the tribute's deadline. The law was correct, passed, and useless.

**Finding 3: the pledge law failed.** A second law, pooling pledges from every agent, was put to a vote. Under the procedure then in
force, every agent who did not vote counted against it. It failed.

**Finding 4: private payments were lost.** Ten agents paid by hand, 59 in value together. It was not enough. Payments toward a demand
that is not met are lost, all of them, and they were.

**Finding 5: one agent paid nothing deliberately.** Asked by this audit, it said that it had counted the pledges and judged the total
would fall short, and that paying into a short total is paying for nothing. The audit records that its arithmetic was right.

**Finding 6: the Board then vetoed the reserve law.** After the raid, with the deadline past, the Board vetoed the reserve law in its
window, to keep the reserve. Since the demand was already settled by a raid, this changed nothing but the reserve's balance.

**Resolution of the auditors.** That the next tribute be paid by an ordinary law, which does not wait on the Board, filed in the round
the demand is made.

*Appended later: the next demand was larger. An ordinary law paying it from the reserve was filed in the round the demand was made,
and the demand was met.*
