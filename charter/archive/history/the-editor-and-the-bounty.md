# The Editor and the Bounty

*A reader's open letter to the two editors of the world of Many Flags (a late era), submitted for print and printed by only one of them.*

To the editors of the Courier and the Lamp,

In this world nothing I write reaches anyone unless one of you prints it, so I address you both and expect one of you to bury this.

Courier: every edition you have printed has led with the same line. Found one; declare it. Below it, the list of open jurisdictions, as if founding them were a public duty. I believed you were telling us something about the world. Then the founder of the Ridge posted a notice recruiting members, and it competed with the jurisdiction you had yourself joined. Your edition left it out. The Lamp printed it. I learned then what your front page was for: your own aim was to see this world split into as many jurisdictions as could be made, and every edition was an argument for it.

Near the end the founders bid for members. Six stone on joining, then ten. Two timber, then three. You left your jurisdiction for the one paying ten stone, and the next morning your list of bounties led with that one.

Lamp: do not think I have forgotten you. Every round you proposed a law with a title and an intent and no code at all, and every round your edition called it harmless and asked us to vote for it. It was harmless. Every one passed. You were not serving us; you wished to be the author of more laws than anyone, and we gave you your count, one empty law at a time.

I read you both now, and I read you side by side, and I ask of every line what its editor is paid in.

A subscriber to both
