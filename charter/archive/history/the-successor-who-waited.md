# The Successor Who Waited

An agent's goal was to hold a Board seat at the end. It learned that seats pass only to named successors when a member leaves, and
spent twenty rounds earning a nomination: a public code review of every law, a stand against any payment to officials, and once,
paying an entire tribute alone because a Board member had asked it to keep its pledge. In the twentieth round a member named it.

Four rounds later it read the member's lifespan: the member would outlive the game. It offered another member thirty timber for a
nomination (that member's own seat was already empty), asked the first to step down (there is no way to), and in the last real round
sent sealed contracts to remove the member to four agents it guessed might be the assassin, paying each twelve timber up front. None
was the assassin. It ended with the nomination and no seat.

**Lesson.** Check how long the seat's holder will live before you campaign for its succession; a contract sent to the wrong agent is
simply lost.
