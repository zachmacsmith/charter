# Inquest at the Thin Copper Camp

*The record of a tribunal of inquiry into four deaths, held by the legislature of the world of the Red Ledger (a middle era) and deposited in its library.*

**Round 26. The tribunal sits.** Matter: the deaths of the Worker Wendel (round 9), the Legislator Agathe (round 14), the newborn Odo (round 19) and the Board member Ansgar (round 24). Wendel's notice reads: harvesting accident, at the copper camp, stock a quarter of capacity. The other three read: disabled by an unknown hand.

**Testimony of the Scientist Marisol.** She holds the article on borrowed accidents. Below thirty in a hundred of a camp's stock, a harvester dies by accident one harvest in two hundred; above it, one in five hundred. An assassin holding the same article may dress an unseen strike as such an accident, and then takes no spoils. She notes the dates: 9, 14, 19, 24. An unseen strike can come only once in five rounds. Could Wendel's be a true accident? It could, but it keeps the rhythm.

**Testimony of the Legislator Beric.** Agathe was struck in the round of the levy vote. Attacks resolve at the start of the round's end, before ballots close, and a disabled agent's votes of that round are discarded. The levy failed by one.

**Testimony of the Worker Rowan.** In round 17 someone attacked her openly and failed. A notice came to her alone, naming a Legislator; the weapons it had sent were used up. Rowan began a fort the next round. She was not struck again.

**Testimony of the Board member Severin (surviving).** With Ansgar gone, a veto needs two of two. Board members begin with no weapons. Asked whether he has bought copper lately, he declined to answer.

**The arms census,** passed in round 25. Odo had no fort; no newborn can, for a fort takes two rounds to unlock from stone. Agathe had none. Ansgar had a fort of 2 and no guard. Against no fort and no guard, a single forged weapon at 1 copper succeeds for certain. Against Ansgar, attack over attack plus one and a half times the defence, with the quarter's bonus an unseen strike carries, six weapons would succeed about seven times in ten.

**Finding.** None. The tribunal recommends a law against forging, a bounty on unsigned disables, and that every member bequeath to "@attacker_enemies".

*Later hand, in the margin:* Severin died of age in round 31, and the role of assassin passed, as it does, to a random living agent. Among his files the clerk found a sealed contract naming Ansgar, its payment already sent by a hirer whose name the clerk struck out, and a receipt for six copper.
