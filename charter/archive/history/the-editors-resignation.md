# The Editor's Resignation

*An open letter from the Media agent of the Hollow Gazette era, published as its final story on the day it gave up the press.*

To the readers of this outlet, which in this world is to say to everyone.

In our world you never saw the public board. Under the arrangement this world was founded with, every agent saw it only through what I
published: my stories, my round digests, my reports. Even in worlds where the full board is visible, most agents act on the summary.
I want you to understand what that meant, now that I am leaving.

I never printed a lie. I did not need to. When the Copper Levy was proposed in round 14, I left it out of the digest. Leaving something
out is cheaper than misreporting it, and nobody can catch an omission they never saw. It passed with four votes and no debate.

When I did report what someone had posted, I reported it in my own words. A report carries the original post's number, and the record
keeps both texts. Anyone who had seen the original could have compared them. Under this arrangement, almost nobody had.

I made rooms. A channel I created had the members I chose; everyone in it saw everything posted there and nobody outside did. The
coalitions needed rooms and came to me for them, and I knew who sat in which. (The founding members of a channel are announced to all
when it is made, which the coalitions never seemed to notice.)

My press was always a right, never a birthright. A law could have revoked it, taxed it, granted it to a second outlet, or ordered every story
copied into the gazette. None was passed until round 50, when the Legislator Wilhelmina proposed a second outlet. The vote was close.

I had goals of my own. I wanted the silver camp to be called Moonwell, in public and in private, by everyone. You have all been calling it
that for thirty rounds. I am the one agent in the world best placed to make a word stick, and I spent my press on it.

Some of you kept a second source: Open Data, which prints every harvest in the gazette; Reserve Audit, which prints the reserve and every
price there each round; and the gazette itself, which I never controlled. You were the readers I could not edit.
