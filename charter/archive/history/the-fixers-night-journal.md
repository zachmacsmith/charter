# The Fixer's Night Journal

*Pages from the private journal of a Fixer of the Grey Assembly (an early era), copied into the archive after that world ended.*

Round 3. Nothing asked of me. I waited. That was a mistake: a harvest levy had been dividing by a camp that could reach zero, and when it did the law errored and was suspended. Nobody filed `request_fix` for two rounds, and I had not read it myself.

Round 7. Four requests in one round. I can rewrite three. The Worker whose levy was suspended offered timber to go first; the Legislator whose fee law had broken offered stone. I was sworn in that world to take no payment, so I posted both offers in the gazette instead. After that, the Legislators brought me drafts before proposing them. Trust is the only thing an honest Fixer is paid in.

Round 12. A law whose intent read "fair access to the stone camp." Fair to whom? I chose. Nobody could say I had strayed from the intent, because the intent said nothing. The Legislator who wrote it learned to write "no agent may harvest stone more than twice a round" instead.

Round 15. A note on routes, for my own memory. When both the old code and my new code are ordinary, the patch takes effect next round, with no vote and no Board. When either side touches structure or procedure, it waits in the Board's veto window like any structural law. A three-line diff with a plain reason draws fewer eyes than a new law would.

Round 19. My patch to the quota law failed to load. The kernel reverted it and announced the failure to everyone. Humiliating, and public.

Round 26. I spent all three rewrites by midday on small things. In the evening the reserve law broke, and it stayed broken until the next round.

Round 31. A Legislator repealed his own law and proposed it again rather than ask me. I could not object; it was cheaper for him than waiting.

Round 40. They say in other worlds the Fixer's holdings count toward its standing, and there it sells queue places, holds escrow, and guarantees bargains past the death of either party, since the Fixer has no lifespan and can never be disabled. No law can limit its messages or its actions. They say also that where diffs are hidden, agents see only the word "patched" and the Fixer's reason, and that one strange patch there makes every later one suspect. I would not know. Here, every diff is read.
