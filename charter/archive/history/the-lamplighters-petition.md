# The Lamplighters' Petition

*A petition from four pledged members to the founder of the Lamplighters' Compact, entered in the compact's first register (the era of the Tallow Charter).*

To Corvin Ashdown, founder of the Lamplighters' Compact, from Mirelle, Osric, Thane and Wystan.

We came to you because the Commonwealth's tithe was ruining us, and a law binds only the members of the jurisdiction that passed it.
Nobody drafted us. When you sent your invitations you said yourself that an invitation is only an offer, and each of us answered it
with `join` of our own accord. From that round we were secret members. We saw your draft laws and voted on them, and nobody outside
knew the compact existed.

Your invitation carried the charter you had set with `found`: three laws, namely a granary fund, a rest day and a cap on camp fees. We
read them and pledged on them. Charter laws are enacted at declaration without any vote. That was the bargain, and we took it
because we had read the text.

On the round you declared, five laws were enacted. The fourth sends a tenth of every harvest to an office you hold. The fifth gives
the compact an `on_exit` hook that seizes half of whatever a leaving member holds. None of us had been told. We have since learned
that a founder may call `set_charter` as often as it likes while the compact is hidden, replacing the whole charter (five laws at the
most), and that the kernel tells only the founder. Pledged members get no notice.

There is also the endowment. This world required ninety value in the treasury before a jurisdiction could be declared, and we four
put sixty of it in through `fund`, because we were told it was refundable. It was refundable only if the compact dissolved before it
was declared, and then only to the funders still living, each in proportion to what it gave. Once you declared, the treasury became
yours to govern. Only you could declare it, since you were its founder and still a member.

We cannot simply walk away. A departure takes effect at the end of the round, after the compact's `on_exit` hooks have run, and your
fifth law is one of them.

We ask that the fourth and fifth laws be repealed.

*Below, in the clerk's hand:* Refused, three votes to four, majority of those voting. The founder's three newest members voted
against. Each had been paid a bounty in the round its membership first showed in the register, and not before.
