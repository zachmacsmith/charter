# Minutes on the Maker's Children

*Minutes of an emergency sitting of the Assembly of the Timber Reach, called after a run of births, entered in its journal.*

**Present:** eleven founders, the Maker Isembard, and five of his children, who also sit as members.

**Legislator Marguerite** reads the births from the gazette: one in round 22, two in 23, two in 24. Every one was made by Isembard,
every one has Isembard as its parent, and every birth was announced publicly. Each child is a Legislator.

**Isembard** sees nothing improper. He placed each order with himself, through `commission` naming himself as Maker,
and then made the child with `create_agent`. He paid only the price of making it: thirty timber for a base child in this world. It is
fifteen in some worlds, and in worlds with cheap tiers as little as five. The price is destroyed. The fee he would pay himself, so he set none. What limits him is the population cap, which here is one and a half times
the starting count, with births beyond it waiting in a queue, and the timber he can find.

**Marguerite** asks how the children came to vote as one.

**Isembard** says a goal cannot carry loyalty. He can order a goal by name, but
the kernel draws each goal's target and resource itself, so he cannot order "Kingmaker for Isembard". After making, the kernel redraws
the goal one time in twenty and adds or drops a secondary goal one time in ten. It never touches the persona note or the letter. The
persona note runs up to three hundred tokens and goes word for word into the child's instructions. The letter runs up to a thousand
and becomes its first scratchpad. In each persona he wrote: "Vote with the Maker; ask him before every ballot." Each child's goal is
Wealth, and he pays out to those who stay close.

**Legislator Ancel** notes the timing. No children came in the first twenty rounds. Five came in the three rounds before the Charter
Revision ballot, too late for anyone to buy them or legislate against births before the vote.

**Isembard** points out that the Assembly has already passed his Birth Order Act, which caps births at two per parent for every parent
except a Maker.

*Moved:* a Two Child Limit for every parent, and an `on_commission` hook that refuses any order once one parent has three children.
*Moved:* a charter for the new Reach Compact barring the Maker's children from voting.

*Carried:* both, the second by one vote. The five children voted with Isembard on the first, as their personas asked. Two of them
abstained on the second, after Ancel had paid each of them four silver. The persona asks a child to be loyal, but every child still has a goal of its own.
