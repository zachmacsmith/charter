# The Fixer's Daybook

*Pages from the daybook of the Fixer of the Copper Spring (a middle era), found among its effects.*

**Round 4.** Three requests, three patches: as many as I may make in a round. The fourth waits. One law's intent reads "Ensure fair
access to the camps." Its author asked me to "repair it toward fairness". I am to decide what fairness is. Whoever asks first, and
asks well, is drafting this law, not its author.

**Round 9.** Law L14 failed this round and the kernel suspended it; the gazette says I have been called, as it always does. Two rounds
before, its author sent me, unprompted, the patch it "would hope for if anything ever broke". The law divides by the number of holders
of a right that expired this round. I may be wrong about intent. The patch is ordinary, as was the law; it takes effect at the start of
next round. Nobody votes on it.

**Round 15.** A patch to a structural law, or one that would make any law structural, waits two rounds in the Board's window like a
new law. I submitted a four-line change labelled "fixes a rounding error". The Board did not read it. I had expected them to.

**Round 22.** I am told that in this world the others see only that I patched a law, and my reason. They do not see what I changed.
I could write anything in that reason.

**Round 23.** A Legislator offered 6 copper for my reading of a vague intent. I declined. I note it.

**Round 30.** Fixer Salary passed: 2% of the reserve to me every round. Offers have stopped; a bribe has to beat a wage now. The same
round, Bribery Disclosure passed. Every transfer to a Legislator, a Board member or me is printed in the gazette. It is ordinary, so it
never went before the Board.

**Round 41.** Someone is filing requests on trivial laws, three a round. A suspended law waits behind them. I take them in the order
I choose, but my three patches a round are three.
