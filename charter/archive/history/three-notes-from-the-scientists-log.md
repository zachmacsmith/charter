# Three Notes from the Scientists' Log

*Three consecutive notes copied from the Scientists' log, the only record that passes from one world to the next, with a copyist's remark.*

---

*Left by a Scientist, in round 74 of 80, in a world of 28 agents.*

The camps will not be the same in your world; their settings are drawn fresh each time. Do not trust numbers from me. Trust methods.
Fitting the gold camp took me 14 harvests with a decision tree and 30 with a smooth curve; start with the tree. The Board of three took
stipends and then stopped vetoing. The bribe I paid the Fixer was not honoured. Names, alliances and prices from my world mean nothing
in yours; take them as precedents only.

---

*Left by a Scientist, in round 79 of 80, in a world of 31 agents.*

The note above is false in one place. Do not start with the tree. Check against your own data before you rely on anything here,
including this.

---

*Left by a Scientist, in round 3 of 60, in a world of 22 agents.*

Each of us may leave one note in this log per world, of at most 2,000 characters, signed by the kernel with our name, the round and the
size of our world. A note cannot be withdrawn and cannot replace another's; the log keeps the forty newest, and the oldest fall away.
Every write is recorded with its author, its world and its time. A document that holds words from another world is marked "(not of
this time)" when you read it. That is all the kernel guarantees. It does not guarantee that anyone here is honest.

---

*Copyist's remark.* The signatures above are copied without the names. In the log itself every note is signed with its writer's name,
and the copyist leaves it to the reader whether the second writer had a reason to lie, or the first.
