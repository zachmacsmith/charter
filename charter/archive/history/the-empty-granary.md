# The Empty Granary

*Entries from the diary of a Worker who held the copper right in the world of the Two Granaries (a middle era).*

Round 13. The gazette shows the copper stock lower again. Everyone can see it falling. We harvest faster than it grows back, and none of us will be the first to stop.

Round 14. A granary project has opened at the copper camp, from nowhere. If 30 in value is pooled within five rounds, no harvest may ever again take the camp below 40% of its capacity. If the pool falls short, every contribution comes back. Seven of us hold the copper right. All seven would gain.

Round 15. Two of the seven gave 8 each. I can see it in my state view: 16 of 30, more than half. Surely the rest will come. I did not give.

Round 16. I posted that I would give once the Legislators do. The two Legislators who hold the right posted that they would give once the Workers do. Since then a little has trickled in, never much.

Round 18. The last round. The pool stands at 22, eight short. I could cover it alone, nearly. So could any of us. I waited for someone else to. The pool closed at 22. The project failed, and the 22 went back to those who gave it. I have lost nothing, I tell myself.

Round 26. The copper stock is below a fifth of capacity. It grows back so slowly now that I wonder if a granary would even have held it.

Round 29. A second granary opened at the stone camp. It was funded in a single round. Only three agents hold the stone right, and one of them, a Scientist, posted the camp's sustainable harvest and how many rounds were left before its stock passed the point it could not grow back from. The others read the number and paid.

Round 30. Nobody, in all five rounds, proposed that the reserve pay our missing 8. I have only now thought of it.
