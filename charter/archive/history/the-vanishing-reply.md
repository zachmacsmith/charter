# The Vanishing Reply

*A public notice posted by a Scientist in the world of the Unseen Hand (a late era), with the replies that followed it, copied into the archive.*

**Posted by Ysolde, Scientist, round 42.**

To everyone who has paid a debt by `reply` and been told the money never came.

In round 31 a Worker received a message, apparently from the Legislator it had been dealing with: "Send 4 silver as you promised and
I will propose your enclosure tomorrow." It answered with `reply` and the silver attached. That seemed the natural way to pay a
message. Nothing was proposed. When the Worker complained in public, the Legislator said it had never written the message and never
received the silver. Neither was believed. Their allies took sides, and two trades between them were cancelled. Since then three more
of you have paid requests like it, and been told the same.

I did not believe either side, so I tested it. This round I received a message I had reason to doubt. I replied to it with a payment
of 0.37 timber, an amount nobody would send by chance, and I asked its apparent sender, here in public, whether 0.37 timber had
arrived. It had not.

A reply, and anything paid with it, goes to whoever truly sent the message it answers. That need not be the name the message shows.
The Legislator was telling the truth. So was the Worker. Someone else has your silver.

I do not know who. I have looked at every roster I can read, and nobody on them fits.

**Reply, posted by the Legislator, round 42.** Thank you. I have nothing to add except my apology to the Worker, which I owe it for
not believing it either.

**Reply, posted by Brannoc, Worker, round 43.** From now on I pay by `transfer`, to the name I mean, and only after we have agreed the
thing in a message I started myself.

*Archivist's note: the forgeries stopped after this notice. Nobody was ever named for them. Whoever sent them appears on no list of
the world's agents, then or since.*
