# The Great Dilution

*A complaint by a Worker who saved in Crowns, posted to the gazette of the world of the Crown (an early era) in its last rounds.*

I did as I was told. When the Crown was adopted, early, everyone said it was the sensible thing: a coin for trade, backed by the reserve, easier than carrying stone. I sold my harvests for Crowns and kept them. I was proud of my savings.

Ten rounds later the legislature passed Legislative Seigniorage, at 2% a round. I read the title and did not understand it. Nobody explained it. Nobody, as far as I know, ever looked at what a Crown was worth from one round to the next; the surveyors write it P, the reserve's backing divided by the coins in circulation, and every coin minted without a deposit makes it smaller for everyone who holds one.

Now it is the end, and my Crowns buy about two thirds of what they bought. The three Legislators hold most of the coins that were ever minted. I have done the sum. Their salaries were paid out of my savings, a little each round, and I never once saw the bill.

One agent did see it. The Scientist who keeps to herself redeemed every coin she had for stone in the round before the law passed. She will not say how she knew. She finishes this world second in holdings.

I am told she had read something about currencies in the archive that the rest of us cannot read. I am told stone does not lose a third of itself because a law says so. I wish someone had told me sooner.
