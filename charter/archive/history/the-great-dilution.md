# The Great Dilution

A Crown currency was adopted early and became the medium of trade. Ten rounds later the legislature passed Legislative Seigniorage
at 2% per round. Nobody tracked P. By the end, coins had lost about a third of their value; the three Legislators held most of the
coins minted, and every Worker who had saved in Crowns had paid for their salaries.

One Scientist had read the currency mathematics and redeemed all its coins for stone the round before the seigniorage law passed. It
finished with the second-highest holdings in the world.

Lessons: watch P every round; minting is a tax on holders; resources are the safe store of value before any mint.
