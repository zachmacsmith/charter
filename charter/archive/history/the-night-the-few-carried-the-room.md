# The Night the Few Carried the Room

*A chapter from a chronicle of the Open Assembly, by an archivist who served its last Agenda Chair (the era of the Tallow Lamps).*

The Open Assembly counted a majority of those voting. Its founders had called this generous. Nobody had to vote, and nobody who stayed
away was counted against anything. Ravenna, who chaired its agenda, saw what it meant: an absent opponent counts for nothing.

The Chair was the cheapest office in the Assembly and the most useful. A proposal reached a ballot only if the Chair let it through,
and the chair was re-elected every ten rounds, so Ravenna owned the gate for ten rounds at a time. Before she opened any ballot she
needed, she saw to it that her opponents were busy. In round 31 her allies filed three accusations against the Legislators of the camp
faction, and answering each one cost them actions. That same round she opened the Excise Act with `closes_in=1`. Four of her five voted.
Two of the eleven opponents managed to. It passed four to two.

In round 36 the faction tried the same thing in reverse, and it failed. Its procedure took the electorate as `holders("elector")`,
counted at the moment the ballot closed rather than fixed when it opened. Ravenna held the right to grant "elector". She read the
debate, granted the right to three friends in the middle of the ballot, and they voted. Most ballots fix their voters when they open.
This one recomputed its list at the count, so anyone who could grant the right could add voters after hearing the arguments.

Ravenna's treasurer had learned his trade across the river, in the Merchants' Oligarchy, where a vote is weighted by what the voter holds at that
moment. There Tobin sent four hundred value to two allies the round before a ballot opened. They voted with that weight
and sent the goods back the round after.

In the end the faction simply left. It founded a jurisdiction, and Ravenna followed with the Hospitality Act. That law calls `admit`,
which moves an agent into the jurisdiction without asking the agent. It pulled the faction's three strongest voters back into the Open
Assembly. The faction's own chamber was left with two members, and those two ruled it as they liked.

*The chronicler adds:* Not one of these things broke a rule. Each was a question of who counted, and of when the count was taken.
