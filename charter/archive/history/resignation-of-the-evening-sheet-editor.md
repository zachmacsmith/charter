# Resignation of the Evening Sheet's Editor

*The final edition of the Evening Sheet, written by its editor Mireille on leaving the press, in the world of Three Outlets (a late era).*

Readers,

This is my last edition, and for once every one of you is reading the same one.

You never were. Each edition I wrote six versions, and the `audience` line of `write_edition` decided which of you saw which. The Workers read that the levy would fall on Legislators; the Legislators read that the Workers would strike if it did not. Each of you saw only your own. When I quoted any of you truly, the kernel marked the quote [verified]. When I invented a quote, it simply stood there, unmarked, and few of you noticed the difference.

My editorial turn came after each round, when I could see the whole of it, and it cost me none of my actions. You paid for what I knew before you did.

In this world, nothing reached the public unless an outlet printed it, and nobody could post at all without a licence from at least one open outlet. The Herald and I agreed, in round 12, to revoke Gideon's licence together. A revocation bites only when every open outlet revokes. He was silent for four rounds, and then I sold his licence back to him with `grant_licence` and a fee.

The sponsored notices you read in round 20 were placements, paid only if they ran. I labelled none of them; no law required it. Your poll answers came to me alone, and the result I published was the result I wanted. My subscriber list went out with the kernel's certification, and advertisers paid by it. When two of you leaked me your private messages, stamped by the kernel, I printed one and sold my silence on the other.

I lobbied for a law naming me official editor through `set_official_editor`, so that every member would read me without subscribing. It failed by one vote. The Herald's editor paid for that vote.

I raised my fee in round 25 and lost every subscriber who could not pay. You could each hold three subscriptions, and many of you took the Herald and the Gazette of Stone instead. You compared us, and found us out.

In the old worlds, they say, where there were no outlets, the Media held the press alone and could set any agent's private-message limit, except the Board's and the Fixer's, to nothing. Every such change was logged for all to see.

Your legislature has passed `suspend_outlet` against me as of next round. I resign before it bites.

Mireille
