# Song of the Copper Diggers

*A work song of the Workers of the copper camp in the world of the Salt Charter, with a chronicler's note appended.*

> The Legislator writes, and the Board says nay,
> The Fixer mends laws by the light of the day,
> But none of them dig, for the Board and the Fixer
> May never take harvest, and nothing comes freer:
> Each ingot, each stone, every coin in the land
> Came up from a camp in a Worker's hand.
>
> Turn the dial, turn the dial, write it down, write it down,
> The x that you chose and the yield that came round,
> The Scientist's sandbox is hungry and blind,
> And she has no right of her own, so be kind,
> But sell her the ledger, and sell it to one,
> And let the other hear that the bidding's begun.
>
> There's two at our camp and there's three at the next,
> And a strike of one digger's a joke, not a threat.
> When all of us down tools, the levies run dry;
> We'll take up our picks for a stipend, or the right to vote aye.
>
> The Board starts out poor, and half his score is his purse;
> Feed him a little each round, and his veto's not ours to curse.
> Lease out the right that you work badly, friend:
> It comes home on its own at the term's end.
>
> Go soft on the camp when the stock's running low,
> For below thirty in a hundred the accidents grow:
> One harvest in five hundred takes a digger outright,
> And one in two hundred when the camp's thin and slight,
> And the hand in the dark that strikes without name
> Can dress up its work as the camp's own blame.

*Chronicler's note.* The song was sung in the round before the Salt legislature passed a harvest quota without consulting the diggers. A quota, like a fee or a harvest limit, is an ordinary law: it took effect at once and never came before the Board, whatever the diggers had paid him. Two rounds later a second law granted the copper right to three Legislators, and a Scientist bought her (x, yield) ledger from one digger alone and published the camp's best dial to the others for nothing. The fourth verse was not sung again.
