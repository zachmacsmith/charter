# The Speculator's Daybook

*Pages from the daybook of a Worker who traded in public works, kept among the papers of the Copperfield era.*

**Round 6.** A road is offered to a new camp, threshold 60 value. Every contributor who gives at least 1 value gets a right at the new
camp, and a contributor of 1 gets the same right as one who gives 40. I gave 1 timber. If the road fails, this one refunds its stakes;
others send them to the reserve. Either way I risk 1 timber.

**Round 9.** The road is funded. Funding is checked at every contribution: the threshold met, the goods
are spent and the camp exists. My right came with it, the same as Brandt's, who gave 38.

**Round 14.** The Assembly passed a law that opens a road and promised to pay for it from the reserve "next round". If nobody gives at
least 1 value, every Worker gets the right. If anybody does, only the contributors get it. I staked 1 timber between the two laws and so
did my sister. When the reserve paid the rest, the new camp had two right holders, and we were they. The Assembly now opens and funds a
road in a single law.

**Round 19.** An expedition is offered. It finds a camp only if the threshold is met and at least 60% of the agents (Board and Fixer
excepted) have each given at least 1 value, which comes to 15 of the 25 of us. Every Worker gets the right whether it gave or not, and so
does every contributor. Fourteen had given. I was the fifteenth, and I let the funders know my price was 4 silver. They paid it.

**Round 23.** I watched the granary at camp3, which a law had opened with `start_project("granary", 20, 5, params={"camp": "camp3"})`.
A granary keeps anyone from harvesting the camp's stock below 40% of capacity. Camp3 stood at 31%. Once the granary was built, nobody
could harvest there at all until the stock grew back. The round it climbed past the floor, whoever came first in the order took the
whole allowance. I was third.

**Round 27.** An upgrade is offered at the commons, yields times 1.5 for twenty rounds. At a commons an upgrade pays only if the holders
cut their harvests. I let the others fund it.

**Round 30.** A granary was offered whose threshold had to be paid in stone. I hold most of the stone, so whether it gets built is my
decision. I sold twenty to the funders at half again the going rate.

*Last line:* A Board member gave 30 to the round-19 expedition and got nothing for it. The Board and the Fixer may contribute, but they never
receive harvest rights.
