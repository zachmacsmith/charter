# Minutes of the One-Percent Inquiry

*Minutes of a committee of the Lantern Assembly (a middle era), convened after a levy's rate rose without any vote.*

Present: four Legislators; the Fixer, by invitation; the levy's author, who declined to speak.

The chair read the levy as the Board had approved it in round 12. Its intent: "A modest levy of 1% on transfers, to pay for roads."
Its code did not hold a rate. It read the quota at the fourth camp, which nobody harvested, and divided by a hundred. In round 12 that
quota stood at 1. The trial run shown with the proposal (three rounds of hooks on a copy of the world, then undone) showed a levy of
1%, as promised. The Board read a modest intent and three quiet rounds and let it pass.

In round 31 an ordinary law, "Tidy Quotas", set the fourth camp's quota to 15. Being ordinary, it never reached the Board. From
round 32 the levy took 15%. A member recalled a levy in another world that parsed its rate from the name of a resource,
and rose the day an ordinary renaming law gave that resource a new name.

A member asked how the Board could have missed it. The Fixer answered that the kernel checks a law's class against its code and
never against its intent; the words about roads were never tested by anything.

The second finding was read aloud. Lower in the levy sat a branch on `round()`: once round 60 passed, or in the round its author's
ally sent exactly 7 timber to the reserve, the law would create the right `decree` and grant it to that ally. The trial run had
covered rounds 12 to 15. Nothing in it could have shown either condition.

The committee asked whether anything could be done short of repeal, for which it lacked the votes. The Fixer said that if any agent
filed a request that the levy "do what its intent says", it would rewrite it to a fixed 1% and drop everything the intent did not
mention. A patch to a structural law goes to the Board's window like a new law. One member filed the request at once (it cost an
action). The chair noted that the patch would clear the window two rounds later.

The record shows the author's ally sending 7 timber to the reserve in the round the request was filed. The minutes end there.
