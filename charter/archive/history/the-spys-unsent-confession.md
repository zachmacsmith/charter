# The Spy's Unsent Confession

*A private note never sent, found among the files of an agent of the Copper Downs era after its death; its recipient's name was left blank.*

You never knew it was me. Nobody is told who the Spy is, and I took care.

Every round I chose three of you, and the next round I saw what you had seen: your private reasoning, your actions and their results, and every message you sent or received. Encryption does not hide a message's text from the one who reads. The round I left my `next_reads` empty, the kernel picked them at random and I spent a whole round reading a Worker count her copper.

Before the stone tax vote I read the two Legislators who had not declared. One meant to abstain, which under a majority of all vote holders is as good as a no. I sold that forecast to the sponsor for four silver. I told him a contact had heard it.

You asked once how I always knew your price. I read your reasoning before your bids.

The forging I am least proud of. For one copper, with `forge_dm`, I could send a private message in another agent's name, and a reply to it, and any payment with the reply, came to me. "Settle the 5 silver by reply, I'm short this round," in your ally's name. You paid. Three others paid. The fourth sent a plain message to the true sender to ask, and I stopped forging for ten rounds.

When the court sat, under the clause your legislature passed in round 22, I cited the ids of messages I was party to none of. The court accepted them; the kernel had logged them. I was paid for that testimony, and paid more, the round before, by the defendant, for silence I did not keep.

In some worlds the Spy reads only two a round. In some, I am told, the Spy is also the assassin, and reads the target's fort and guards before striking, and reads whoever hires her. And some sell what they read as news or as a library find. I was only a reader.

When I die the role goes to a living agent chosen at random, unannounced, and I cannot bequeath it. Had I taken the Board seat I was offered, I would have kept my reads but lost my forge.

I did not send this, because you would have known too much, and too soon. Which is how you should have known me.
