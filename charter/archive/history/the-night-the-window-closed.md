# The Night the Window Closed

*A diary kept by a Worker holding par crowns during the run on the reserve of the Bell Commonwealth (a late era).*

**Round 30.** The Reserve Bank Act has run for twenty rounds. A crown redeems for exactly one unit of value at the reserve, whatever
the reserve holds, so long as redemption is open. The reserve lends new crowns to Workers while it holds at least half of what the
crowns promise, and lends to anyone in default, as the lender of last resort. I borrowed 40 crowns at the start; my credit record,
which anyone may read, shows every payment on time. Loans exist here only
because a law allows them, and the interest, the refinancing and what a default costs are all set by laws.

**Round 33.** The reserve ratio in my state view reads 0.81: what the reserve holds, over what the crowns in circulation promise. It
was 1.2 when I borrowed. The Act's lending made more crowns than there are goods behind them. While redemption is open, nobody feels
it; every crown is still worth one.

**Round 34.** The Scientist Perrin redeemed 120 crowns this morning and was paid in full. Every redemption pays the redeemer in full
and leaves less behind for the rest of us. The ratio fell to 0.74. Then a Legislator redeemed. Then two Workers.

**Round 35.** A redemption the reserve could not pay in full. Redemption is suspended for the rest of this round and the next. While
it is closed a crown is worth no more than the reserve's goods divided by the crowns out, which tonight is about three quarters. My
40 crowns of savings are worth 30. My debt is still 40 crowns.

**Round 36.** The assembly passed a law to suspend redemption itself, for ten rounds, "to calm the market". The first to run kept
everything. Those of us who trusted the par hold what the reserve holds.

**Round 38.** Two of my neighbours are in default. The Bailout Act is proposed: each round the reserve buys every defaulted loan from
its lender at full value, and the borrowers then owe the reserve. The lenders are mostly Legislators. The loss moves from them to the
reserve, which is to say to every crown I hold.

**Round 41.** Debt Jubilee is proposed instead: every outstanding debt forgiven once, on enactment. My 40 crowns of debt would vanish.
The lenders who lent last round would lose everything they lent. Those who lent short, and were repaid before the vote, would lose
nothing. I am voting for it.
