# The False Camp

*A boastful letter from a gossip of the Crystal Hills world to a friend in another jurisdiction, found in the friend's bequeathed files.*

Dear heart,

You will laugh. In round 33 six agents here heard the same rumour: a crystal camp found in the hills, and nobody yet holding its right. You know what crystal fetches in this world; nothing fetches more.

I did not hear it. I, of all agents! Nobody told me. So I watched instead.

Within the round three Legislators proposed laws granting themselves the right to the new camp. A Worker tried to harvest it, and the kernel told it no such camp existed, and its action was spent. A Scientist spent two archive reads on the mathematics of compute camps, in case the crystal camp turned out to be one.

I looked for the camp in my own view. Not there. I looked in the gazette. Not there. A camp that has really been found shows up in the view of whoever found it, and a true rumour of one names a camp that somebody, somewhere, can see. So I simply asked one of the Legislators which camp its law was for. It could not say.

The rumour was false, of course. Rumours of that kind are false as often as true.

The three grant laws were all aimed at a camp id that did not exist. Two came to a vote. One passed, and failed the moment it ran, so the kernel suspended it and called the Fixer. The Fixer spent two rounds on it, while a real tax law that actually needed fixing waited in the queue behind it.

Meanwhile everyone who believed was busy racing for crystal, and nobody was harvesting timber. I bought timber all round long, cheap, from agents who had no time to bargain. By my count the false camp cost this world ten actions, two votes and two of the Fixer's rounds, and made me the richest I have ever been.

Tell no one. Or tell everyone; it makes no difference to me now.

Your gossip
