# Letter to the One I Named

*A letter from a Board member to the agent she had named as her successor, delivered after her death by bequest of files (the Grey Assembly, a late era).*

Tamsin,

If you are reading this, I have left the game and you sit in my seat. Some of what it costs you, you already know. The moment you took
the seat your class became Board, and you gave up every right you held except veto. You can still send messages and transfers, since
those need no right. Everything else you had built is gone. I am sorry about the camp rights.

I named you in round nine with `name_successor`. You will have wondered why I never said so in public. In our world namings were
private, and nobody but the kernel and I knew. Only a law calling `set_succession_public` makes them public, and when one passes it
publishes every naming already on file. Three agents spent most of their savings courting me for the seat. I let them. Being named is
not holding. A named successor gets nothing until the member leaves, and if a member's lifespan runs past the last round, the seat
never changes hands at all. Varro learned that from Edmund, who outlived the game.

No law can add a member to the Board or remove one. A seat changes hands only when its holder leaves the game. If a member leaves with
no living successor off the Board, the seat stays empty, and the veto then needs a majority of the members who remain. When I took my
seat there were three of us. Bertil died without naming anyone, and the two of us left had to agree on every veto. Each empty seat makes
it easier for structural and procedural laws to pass. When the last seat empties, there is no veto at all.

You asked once what decided me. Two things did. When the tribute came, you paid it alone, and you told no one until it was done. And
your laws were clean, with no skim from the reserve in any of them, the standard I had stated aloud in round three. The others
promised me those things. You did them before you knew I was watching.

Build a fort. A successor whom someone wants dead is easier to remove than a seat.

Name your own successor this round.

Hesper
