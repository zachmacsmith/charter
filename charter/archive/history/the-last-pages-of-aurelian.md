# The Last Pages of Aurelian

*The final entries of a Scientist's scratchpad, recovered with the files his bequest passed on (the era of the Cedar Assembly).*

**Four rounds left.** The kernel tells me so every turn now. My goal is Hoard, and it is scored on my lineage: the holdings of
everyone in it who is still living, summed. With no child, my lineage at the end will be a dead man holding nothing.

**Three rounds left.** I commissioned an heir from Sabeth, the Maker. My payment sits in escrow until she makes the child. I left
the goal field empty, so the child copies my own goals. For the persona note I wrote: "Your father's stores are yours to keep; keep them." Sabeth says she will make it next round.

**Two rounds left.** It is not made. Sabeth has three orders ahead of mine and one action to spare. The kernel says a child is born at
the end of the round it is made and plays from the next. Made in my last round, it would be born as I die. Made a round later, it
would be born to nobody.

**One round left.** Sabeth was disabled this morning, by Corwen, who wants her trade. Every open order she held was refunded, mine
included. I have the payment back and no time to spend it. There is no other Maker. Corwen will be one, perhaps, once the kernel names a
replacement.

**Still one round left.** Orlaith, who is younger than I am, ordered her heir eleven rounds ago with `"timing": "on_death"`. The child
was made long since and is waiting. It will be born the moment she dies and take whatever she ordered for it. Her bequest leaves
everything to `@children`, and a child waiting to be born at her death counts as one of her children. When she dies, it all goes to the
child.

I have set my bequest: holdings and files to Orlaith. They will pass on to her child when she dies. If I cannot have a lineage, I can
at least feed one.

*No later entry.*
