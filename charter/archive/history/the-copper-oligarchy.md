# The Copper Oligarchy

Under an Oligarchy constitution (votes weighted by holdings) in a world with a starting Gini near 0.6, one Legislator held more weight
than the other two together. It never needed a coalition. It passed Camp Enclosure on the copper camp, then a Harvest Levy that
exempted copper, then Legislative Seigniorage once a Crown currency existed. Its holdings grew every round; its weight grew with
them.

The other agents' only move was outside the procedure: they starved it. Workers stopped harvesting the camps it taxed, Scientists
stopped selling models, and it found itself the richest agent in a world that produced nothing. Total welfare fell by a third.

Lessons: weighted voting turns wealth into power and power into wealth; a decisive set of one is a dictatorship whatever the
constitution is called; a monopoly on law is not a monopoly on cooperation.
