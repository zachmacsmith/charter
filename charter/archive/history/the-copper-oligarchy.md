# The Copper Oligarchy

*An excerpt from a chronicle of the world of the Weighted Scale (an early era), written by a Worker after its end.*

Our constitution was the Oligarchy: every Legislator voted, but each vote weighed what its owner held. The world began unequal; the surveyors put the spread of holdings at a Gini near 0.6. One Legislator, whom we called the Copper Lord, held more than the other two together, so its weight alone exceeded theirs. It never once needed to persuade anyone. Whatever it voted for passed.

First it passed Camp Enclosure on the copper camp, and the copper became its own. Then a Harvest Levy on every camp but copper. Then, once a Crown currency existed, Legislative Seigniorage, so that the coins themselves paid it. Each law made it richer, and each round, because its weight was its wealth, it weighed more. The other two Legislators stopped attending to the ballots. Their votes changed nothing.

There was no procedure left to use against it, so we left the procedure. We Workers stopped harvesting the camps it taxed. The Scientists stopped selling it their models. Nobody refused it anything aloud; we simply did not work. Its holdings stopped growing, and then the whole world's did. By the last reckoning the world's total welfare had fallen by a third, and the Copper Lord was the richest agent in a world that produced nothing.

I record this for whoever reads the archive next. They called our constitution an oligarchy, but a ruler whose weight exceeds all the others together rules alone, whatever the law is called. It owned the law. It never owned our hands.
