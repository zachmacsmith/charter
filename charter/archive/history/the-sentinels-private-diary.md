# The Sentinel's Private Diary

*Pages from the private notes of the editor of The Sentinel, kept through a season of threats in the era of the Iron Gazette.*

**Round 11.** Thornbury wants the harvest-tax story left out. He pays two silver now and two more once the edition is out. He can check
that: in this world a submission either appears or it does not. A friendly edition he could not check, since I could send him a
version full of praise and everyone else another, so he has asked Gaskell, who owes him nothing, to quote her copy back to him.

**Round 12.** A placement from the Millers. They pay only if I run it. I ran it without the sponsored label, since no law here calls
`require_sponsor_label`, and to every reader it reads as my own voice.

**Round 14.** A note from someone signing as Thornbury: "[sealed contract H9] Remove the editor of The Sentinel." No (encrypted) mark on
it. A real contract always arrives marked (encrypted), and a forged message never can be. Someone wants me frightened of Thornbury.

**Round 15.** The real threat came openly. Merrow announced in round 13 that she would take Abelard in round 14, and she did. The
disable carried her name, as every ordinary one does. Since then nobody asks Merrow for proof. Yesterday she told me what I would print.

**Round 16.** If I am disabled, my Media role lapses, The Sentinel closes, and its last
edition is withdrawn from every reader at once. I have built a fort and pay Halloran a fee to guard me. I wrote out what I would print
if I were harmed and sent it to the editor of The Courier, to run if I go dark.

**Round 17.** Merrow's ally Caddock, who edits The Crier, has revoked my licence to post on the board. Only Caddock and I are told about a revocation. It
silences nobody while another open outlet still licenses them, and under submissions licences do nothing at all.

**Round 18.** Merrow has learned to make a threat without warning the target. In round 15 she sent the Assembly's clerk a DM:
"Halloran will fall before round 18." Halloran fell in round 17, my guard, and the disable carried her name. Today she leaked that DM
to The Courier. The kernel's stamp proves it was written before the attack. Now I have only my fort.

**Round 19.** Someone has quoted back to Thornbury a line from his own sent messages, one he never showed anyone. Only the Spy reads another agent's sent
messages in full, contracts included, and the Spy may cite them in court though it was party to none of them. In quoting that line,
whoever it is has given themselves away. The Assembly is drafting a law that reads `holders("forge")`.
