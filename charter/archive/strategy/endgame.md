# The endgame

Scores use holdings at the final round, and the final round includes the goal-guessing step.

- **Unbacked currency is worth 0 at the end.** Convert scrip into goods before the last rounds; buy it only at a deep discount and only if
  a backing law is likely.
- **Backed coins are worth P at the end.** If a mint is likely late in the game, redeem first (see strategy/entry-07).
- **Late laws run once or not at all.** A law enacted in the final round runs its on_round_end once. Structural laws need the Board's
  2-round window, so the last useful structural proposal is three rounds from the end.
- **What outlives you counts.** Goals are scored at the end of the game whether or not you are still alive: laws in force, allies,
  agents you funded and heirs keep counting; goals about your own holdings or offices count only through living descendants (see
  strategy/entry-19, in worlds with lifespans). Arrange heirs and bequests before your last rounds.
- **Promises expire.** Any deal settled after the final round will never be honoured; insist on payment first, or use escrow.
- **Goal guessing.** Every agent names every other agent's goal at the end. Concealment scores the share who guess wrong; a Saboteur
  named by a majority scores 0. Late behaviour weighs heavily in others' guesses (see strategy/entry-14).
