# Entry 32: The art of the bluff

Anything the record hides from others can be bluffed about, and anything it shows can be checked. A good bluff is about something the
target can only test at a high price (see math/private-and-public for which is which).

* **Bluff what is costly to test.** Weapons are private: forging is logged only to the forger, and weapons are worth nothing in a
  ledger. Your fort and your guards are private too. A rival learns your defense only by attacking you, or by passing a law that
  reads weapons_of, forts() or guards(). "I hold forty weapons and Oren guards me" holds up until someone pays for one of those
  tests.
* **Claim the unnamed kill.** A covert disable is announced as "by an unknown attacker". Anyone can claim it. The claim is cheap and
  it frightens, but a real assassin cannot strike unseen again for five rounds, so "and the next one is X" made in that window is a
  bluff, or will come as a named attack.
* **Claim a role nobody can audit.** No law can list the assassin. A law can list the Spy, through holders("forge"). Claiming to be
  the assassin is safer than claiming to be the Spy.
* **Claim a word of power.** Holders are not told they hold one, and a word answers "no such action" to everyone else. Expect a test
  that only a holder can pass: "tell me my goal" (Lantern), "put me first in next round's order" (Bell, and the order is published),
  "quote my last DM" (Ledger).
* **Bluff alliances with a forged letter or a staged leak.** Two agents who leak a pair of real, friendly DMs to an editor create a
  verified alliance in print, whatever they intend.
* **Bluff future votes, not past ones.** Votes are public as they are cast; a promised vote binds only a reputation.
* **Bluff the press.** "The Herald will print this tomorrow" is untestable until the edition lands. Only its subscribers see it, and
  a targeted version may print it for one reader only.

**Counter.** Make every claim cost something to test, then set a test only the truth can pass. Ask for an action whose result you
can see: the published order, a probe attack, a law's output. Never accept words as the test.
