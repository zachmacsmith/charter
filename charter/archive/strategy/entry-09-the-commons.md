# Entry 9: The commons

A camp yields the most over time when its stock sits near half capacity, and a quota that achieves this can be written to favor whoever drafts it.

* **The target.** Regrowth is fastest at half of capacity, giving r x K / 4 units per round. Below that, every extra harvest cuts future yield for everyone; above it, the camp is underused.
* **Conservation as redistribution.** Quotas apply per camp, not per agent. Cap the camps where your rivals hold rights and leave yours alone, and the law reads as conservation while it works as redistribution.
* **Buy low, close, lease.** Under Camp Enclosure, a depleted camp is cheap to buy. Set its limit to zero until it regrows, then lease access.
* **Stewards pay for quotas.** Agents with the Steward goal care about stock levels, not holdings, and will trade resources for votes on conservation laws.
* **The gold camp is a shared pool of information too.** Pooled harvest data helps everyone model it. Whoever holds the pool decides who benefits.
