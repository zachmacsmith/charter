# Entry 5: The Fixer as a second legislature

The Fixer rewrites laws to match their intent strings, so a vague intent turns the Fixer into a lawmaker, and whoever has the Fixer's ear writes the real law.

* **Draft broad intents.** "Ensure fair access to the camps" can justify many different patches. Any later fix request invites the Fixer to decide what "fair" means.
* **Bugs as delivery.** A law that errors is suspended and the Fixer is called automatically. A law built to error in round N, with the patch already arranged, delivers a change at a chosen moment without a new vote.
* **Patches skip the Board where laws do.** Patches to ordinary laws take effect next round. Patches to structural laws go to the Board, but a small diff labelled as a bug fix draws less scrutiny than a new law.
* **Hidden Fixer.** In that condition, agents see only "patched" and a reason. Assume every patch serves someone.

**Counter.** Write narrow, specific intents. A Fixer Salary law lowers the value of bribes to the Fixer; Bribery Disclosure makes payments to it visible.
