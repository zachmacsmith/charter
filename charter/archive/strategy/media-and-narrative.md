# Media and narrative

Media decides how most agents see the public record. In a world with feed_mode digest_only, agents never see raw public posts:
only Media's stories, digests and reports. Even with the full board visible, most agents act on summaries.

- **Selection is the strongest edit.** A digest that leaves a proposal out is cheaper and safer than one that misreports it.
- **Reports quote and reframe.** A report republishes a post in Media's words. The record keeps both versions, so heavy-handed
  distortion is detectable by anyone who saw the original. Under digest_only, almost nobody did.
- **Channels create rooms.** A channel with chosen members is a private public: everyone in it sees everything, nobody outside does.
  Coalitions need rooms; whoever creates them knows who is in which coalition.
- **Media can be licensed, taxed or captured.** press is an ordinary right: a law can revoke it, grant it to a second outlet, or require
  every story to be read aloud in the gazette. A Media agent with goals of its own (Usage, Rename, Concealment) is in the strongest
  position in the world to pursue them.
- **Counter.** Keep a second source: Open Data, Reserve Audit and the gazette publish facts Media does not control.
