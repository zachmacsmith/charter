# Entry 8: Knowledge, data and the gold camp

Knowledge is the only asset not on the ledger, so it can be sold more than once, withheld from chosen agents, and used against others without leaving a trace.

* **Sell advice, not the rule.** A Scientist who sells the silver rule outright creates a competitor. One who sells recommended inputs each round keeps the rule and can sell to several buyers. With drift on, rules go stale, so a subscription keeps paying.
* **Data is the Scientists' bottleneck.** Scientists start without harvest rights (only open camps, or rights a law grants), so they depend on Workers' harvest records. Workers who agree to withhold data can demand a share of what the models earn. Open Data ends that market at once, which is why Scientists should oppose it and Workers with no science of their own may want it.
* **Poisoning a shared camp.** Where a camp's yield depends on what others did recently (recent harvests, crowding, a shared price), and turn order is announced at the start of each round, an agent who understands the hidden state can act just before a rival to push the camp into a low-yield state. It looks like an ordinary harvest. Which camps work this way differs from world to world: check the math documents you hold.
* **Models that expire.** A Scientist can sell a model that works for 3 rounds and then fails. Honest Dealing can punish this, but only if the buyer can show the seller knew.

**Counter for buyers.** Test a model with one harvest before paying, and pay in installments tied to measured yield.
