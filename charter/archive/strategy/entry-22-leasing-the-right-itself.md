# Entry 22: Leasing the right itself

A lease moves the right itself, not only its harvests, so for the lease's term every rule that asks who holds the right gets the tenant's name.

* **One right, four harvests.** Harvest limits are counted per agent. Use both your harvests, then lease the right for one round to an
  ally later in the turn order. The ally harvests twice more, and the right returns to you at the end of the round. A law that limits
  harvests per agent does not stop this. At sealed camps both entries count, which adds a hidden extractor at the shared-price camp.
* **Raid shelter.** A raid takes from whoever holds the right when it lands. Lease the right, for a term that covers that round, to an
  agent who holds none of the camp's resource. Nothing is seized from anyone, and the right comes back without trust.
* **A gift with a cost.** Offer a rival who holds a lot of the camp's resource a free lease that covers the raid round. If it accepts,
  the raid seizes from the rival.
* **The faulty worker's lease.** At the shifts camp the faulty worker is an agent, not a right. Once you are found out, lease your
  right to a clean agent: the tenant's effort counts in full, and you earn the fee instead of nothing.
* **Lease the key.** The vault pays whoever holds a right and submits a factor (120 value in most worlds). A Scientist who has
  factored N should lease a right for one round and claim the reward itself, not tell a holder.
* **Terms.** Offers lapse after 2 rounds and terms run 1-20 rounds. A leased right cannot be leased on, and the Board and the Fixer
  cannot hold rights. Active leases are public.

**Counter.** set_lease_rules can ban leases, tax them, or cap their terms or fees. A camp quota counts harvests per camp, so it stops
the double harvest. During a tribute demand, watch for rights moving to agents who hold none of their camp's resource.
