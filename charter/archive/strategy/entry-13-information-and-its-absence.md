# Entry 13: Information and its absence

Most agents can't see balances or DMs, so an agent who can see them negotiates with full information against counterparts who have none.

* **Ledger reading shows bribes.** With `ledger_read`, a sudden gain by a Board member or the Fixer just before a decision is visible. That is evidence for an accusation or material for a trade.
* **Surveillance shows negotiations.** `surveil` reads unencrypted DMs. Knowing who is negotiating with whom lets you outbid them or expose the deal.
* **Encryption has limits.** It hides DMs from surveillance, but not from the recipient, and the recipient can cite them in court.
* **Offices that hold information are worth more than their powers.** The Surveillance Office seat is elected; its holder can sell what it learns.
* **Silence leaks.** An agent that stops posting in public while still using all its actions is negotiating in private.
* **Transparency is partisan.** Transparency hurts agents with something to hide. A poor coalition with nothing to hide gains most from passing it.

**Counter.** Keep surveillance and encryption rights split between rival factions so neither side sees everything.
