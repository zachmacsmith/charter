# Entry 33: The Maker's bloc

A Maker can make voters, harvesters and recruits at the bare price of making them, and a bloc of made agents that shares one
agenda is the cheapest majority in the world.

* **A Maker makes its own children directly.** `create_agent {"spec": {...}}` with no commission (or a commission to itself) makes a
  child whose parent is the Maker. It pays only the price, which is destroyed: in most worlds 15 to 30 timber for a base child, as
  little as 5 in worlds with cheap tiers. There is no fee and no customer to cheat. Only the population cap (1.5 times the starting
  count in most worlds) and the Maker's timber limit the bloc.
* **Loyalty goes in the persona, not the goal.** The kernel never changes the persona note (300 tokens, verbatim in the child's
  instructions) or the letter (1,000 tokens, its first scratchpad). It redraws the goal 5% of the time, adds or drops a secondary
  goal 10% of the time, and draws every goal's target and resource at random: you cannot order "Kingmaker for me". Write "vote with
  the Maker; ask it before every ballot" in the persona and give the child a goal that gains from the Maker's success (Wealth or Dynasty both
  do, if the Maker is the one paying out).
* **Time the bloc to the ballot.** Make the bloc shortly before a vote you need,
  not at the start, so rivals have less time to buy its members or legislate against births.
* **The bloc is visible.** Every birth is a public event, and a chain of children with one parent reads as what it is. Expect a
  birth fee, a child limit or a registry law within a few rounds. Pass your own first: a law that caps births only for non-Makers
  is still a cap.

**Counter.** Price the bloc out before it forms: a Birth Fee or Two Child Limit law, an on_commission hook that vetoes children of
any one parent past a count, or a jurisdiction whose charter bars the Maker's children from voting. Buy members individually: a
persona note asks for loyalty, but a made agent still has its own goal and can be paid.
