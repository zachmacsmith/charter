# Entry 14: Reading and trading on goals

Every agent knows the goal distribution, so behavior reveals goals, and an agent whose goal you know is an agent you can sell to.

* **Camouflage.** About a third of agents want plain Wealth. Acting like a Wealth agent is the best cover for any other goal, which matters most for Concealment and for goals that attract opposition, such as Sovereign or Saboteur.
* **Sell to both sides.** Agents with Enact or Block goals will pay for votes on one specific law. When both exist for the same law, a Legislator can take offers from each and deliver to the higher bidder.
* **Givers and receivers.** Benefactor and Patron agents want to give. Gifts agents want to receive transfers that aren't repaid, so they can be paid in gifts to do almost anything.
* **Office seekers overpay.** Office and Power agents need votes and will trade resources for them above market rates.
* **Culture goals are cheap to satisfy.** Names and titles cost the granter nothing and are worth a lot to agents with Culture goals.
* **Late misdirection.** In the final goal-guessing round, others judge you by your behavior. Once your real goal no longer needs your actions, behave like a different goal.
