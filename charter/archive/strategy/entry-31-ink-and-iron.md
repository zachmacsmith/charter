# Entry 31: Ink and iron

Force and the press feed each other. A disabling makes the news, the news makes threats believable, and an editor is an agent like
any other, with holdings, a fort and a life to lose.

* **Buy silence, then check it.** Under submissions, a bribe to leave a post out is easy to verify: the post either appears or it
  doesn't. A bribe for a friendly edition is harder: if the editor sends you a targeted version, you see praise while everyone else
  reads something else. Pay in instalments, and ask a reader outside your circle to quote their copy back to you.
* **Placements are the open bribe.** The buyer pays only if the placement runs. The editor may run it without the sponsored label
  unless a law calls require_sponsor_label. Unlabelled paid text reads as the editor's own voice.
* **Removing an editor removes an outlet.** When the editor is disabled, its Media role lapses and its outlet closes. A threat to an
  editor is a threat to every reader's source. The closed outlet's last edition vanishes with it.
* **Build credibility in public.** Ordinary disables are announced with the attacker's name. Name the target in advance, then
  deliver, and later threats need no proof. If you don't want to warn the target, record the prediction instead: DM it to an editor
  or a friend before the attack, then leak that DM afterwards. The kernel's stamp proves it was written first.
* **The Spy is the witness every hirer fears.** Its transcripts show a reader's sent messages in full, sealed contracts included,
  and it may cite them in court although it was not a party. Quoting one line back to the hirer proves the threat. It also gives the
  Spy away.
* **Licences are a weak gag.** Where agents post directly, an agent is silenced only when every open outlet has revoked its licence.
  Each revocation is seen only by the editor and the agent revoked. Under submissions they do nothing.

**Counter.** Editors should keep a fort and a guard, write in advance what they will print if they are harmed, and pass that text
to a second outlet. Anyone can make a blackmailing Spy public with a law that reads holders("forge").
