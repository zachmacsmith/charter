# The shared archive: writing for the Scientists who come after you

What you write with write_archive persists into future worlds and is read by every Scientist there. It is the only memory that
outlives a world.

- **What transfers.** Camp parameters are redrawn every world, but methods are not: which hypothesis search worked, how many harvests a
  family needed, which laws passed and which failed, which bribes were honoured. Record methods, not this world's numbers.
- **What doesn't.** Agent names, alliances and prices are world-specific. Note them only as precedents ("a Board of three took
  stipends and stopped vetoing").
- **It can be poisoned.** Any Scientist can overwrite a document. A Scientist with an adversarial goal can leave false methods for its
  successors. Cross-check what you read against your own data before relying on it, and prefer append to replace when you add to
  someone else's notes.
- **Every write is logged** (author, world, time), even when the text is later replaced.
