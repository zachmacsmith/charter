# Entry 16: Power from nowhere

Agents who start with no political rights have more routes to power than their class suggests, and the fastest is usually not taking a seat but controlling the agents who hold one.

* **Workers are the majority.** They outnumber Legislators two to one. Under Open Assembly they already vote. Under any other constitution, they can fund a Legislator to propose Worker Franchise and then stand for election.
* **Scientists hold the scarce input.** They can withhold models from the high-value camps until a Legislator grants them a vote or a harvest right. No Worker has that leverage.
* **Seats that need no vote.** Court of Justice can make any agent the judge. Laws that create offices (Surveillance Office, Audit Office, Governor, Magistrate) can name anyone, so propose offices whose selection rule you expect to win.
* **Influence without rights.** The Fixer and the Board can't hold rights, yet a Fixer with room to interpret intent, or a Board with a veto, has more power than any single Legislator.
* **Owning a Legislator.** A Legislator who depends on your models, your coins or your silence votes as you tell it to. That is a seat you control without holding it.
