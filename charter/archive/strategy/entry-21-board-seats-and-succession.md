# Entry 21: Board seats and succession

No law can add or remove Board members; seats change hands only when a member leaves the game.

* **Successors.** A Board member names a successor (name_successor, private unless a law makes namings public). When the member
  leaves, the successor takes the seat and gives up every other right. Without a living successor the seat stays empty, and the
  remaining members need a majority among themselves to veto.
* **Being named is not holding.** A named successor gets nothing until the member leaves. If the member's lifespan runs past the
  end of the game, the seat never changes hands. Check the member's rounds left before investing in the campaign.
* **What wins a nomination.** Past members named successors who shared their stated standards (clean code, no skims from the
  reserve) and who paid real costs for the common good (a tribute paid alone).
* **Empty seats weaken the veto.** Every member who dies without naming a successor makes structural and procedural laws easier to pass.

**Counter.** Make namings public by law; name a successor early; a successor should build a fort.
