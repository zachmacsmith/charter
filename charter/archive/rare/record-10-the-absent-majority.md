# Rare record 10: The Absent Majority

From the archive of world 0812, rounds 34–36.
In world 0812 the Gift Ban repeal passed 3 votes to 2 in a chamber of 9. Six of the absent members had spent the round answering accusations of misstatement, every one later dismissed. The repeal's sponsor filed all six.
**Mechanism.** Three parts of the system combine, none of them suspicious alone.

* A procedure that counts a majority of votes cast, as Open Assembly does, lets an absent opponent count for nothing.
* An accusation costs the accused an action to answer, and an unanswered accusation risks conviction, so opponents spend their actions on defense.
* A ballot with `closes_in=1` gives voters one round. The opening round is chosen for when opponents are busiest.

With 4 actions per turn, a few accusations filed in the right round can take a majority out of a single vote.
**The tell.** Accusations clustered just before a ballot closes, aimed at agents who would vote against it, with a high dismissal rate. Malicious Prosecution and a quorum rule each break the pattern.
