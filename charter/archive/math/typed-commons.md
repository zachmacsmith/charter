# Typed camps: the arithmetic of stock

**Which camps are commons.** Four kinds of camp pay in proportion to their stock (yield x S/K): the steady-dial camp, the landscape,
the shared-price camp and the two-sided choice. The other kinds (workshop, shifts, reactor, station, booth, vault) pay fixed amounts,
drawn from a stock too large ever to run short.

**Capacity.** A steady-dial or landscape camp starts with K = 2hy/r. Here h is the camp's number of right holders at the start, y is a
perfect harvest at full stock (in most worlds 8 timber at the steady-dial camp and 1 silver at the landscape), r the regrowth rate.

**Equilibrium.** Let E be the total number of harvests in a round, each counted by its quality (1 at the best input) and multiplied by
any upgrade. The stock settles at s* = S/K = 1 - E/(2h), and the camp then pays yEs* per round in total.
- E = h (one perfect harvest per holder per round): s* = 1/2 and the total is hy/2, the most the camp can pay forever.
- E = 2h (every holder uses both harvests perfectly): s* = 0. A camp at zero never regrows.

**Upgrades.** An upgrade (x1.5) multiplies E by 1.5. Starting from E = h, it lowers s* to 1/4 and the long-run total from 0.5hy to
0.375hy. The upgrade gains only if holders cut their harvests by a third.

**Investment.** Each stone invested is destroyed and adds 2 to K and 0.002 to r (r is capped at 0.4). S grows in proportion, so
the stock fraction and today's yields are unchanged. The gain comes later, as s* = 1 - Ey/(rK) rises.

**Turn order and granaries.** Every harvest in a round is scaled by the stock at the start of that round, so going first earns nothing
per harvest. Order matters only when a cap binds. A granary allows S - 0.4K in total per round, first come first served. A harvest
beyond that pays 0 and still counts as a harvest. A granary at a camp below its floor stops all harvesting until the stock regrows
above the floor.
